# Source-only corner fairing proposal

This experiment starts from the existing `vesselforge.profile_catalog.build_profile("bowl")` and `build_profile("jar")` defaults. It leaves the catalog, prior native adapter, prior STEP files, and prior outputs untouched. It writes only a mathematical design audit and an actual meridian overlay plot. It does not import CadQuery/OCP or create a native candidate.

Each eligible interior vertex is replaced by one quintic Bezier whose endpoints lie at 12% of the shorter adjacent segment length from the corner. Each endpoint has two additional controls collinear with its incoming or outgoing segment, at 25% and 50% of that trim. All six controls therefore lie on the two trimmed source segments; the Bezier convex-hull property keeps the curve within their local convex hull. The controls give a nonzero endpoint tangent matching the line and zero endpoint curvature, which establishes geometric C2 at each faired join for a regular curve. It does not establish shared-parameter C2 for the particular line/Bezier parameter spans.

Original segments shorter than 2.0 mm and turns of 170 degrees or more are rejected. A candidate is rejected if combined trims consume 25% or more of any segment. The two defaults are checked with eight subdivisions per quintic, for no more than 256 curve-parameter evaluations or contour sample vertices combined. Each quintic is analytically regular for all parameters because its derivative control vectors have strictly positive projection on the tangent bisector; a conservative finite curvature upper bound follows from the control polygon's first and second differences. Sampled curvature values are diagnostics. Profile/section self-intersection and the inner/outer contour separation use bounded polyline samples for these two defaults, not a generic or exact global certificate.

The open-profile endpoints at the axis/floor and both lip endpoints are protected, preserving the axis, bottom and inner-floor datum, clear mouth diameter, and total height. A unique bowl maximum-radius apex is also protected, so that one sharp corner remains in the bowl. The jar's maximum-radius span remains after adjacent trims. All retained sharp corners are listed in `DESIGN_AUDIT.json`.

The outside and inside profile are faired independently. The catalog wall declaration remains in the metadata but does not imply an unchanged realized wall: local and global normal-wall impacts are unknown. The result is not approved for fracture training and has no physical, material, real-vessel, native CAD, STEP, or solver qualification.
